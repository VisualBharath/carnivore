## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a sensory, high-contrast night-drive culinary experience. Rooted in the visual tension between deep volcanic embers and midnight asphalt, it serves hungry travelers, road-trippers, and steakhouse enthusiasts seeking precision routing and uncompromising culinary quality.

The core aesthetic synthesizes modern utility with atmospheric warmth:
- **Atmospheric Obsidian**: Backgrounds are grounded in abyssal dark slates, eliminating digital glare while amplifying high-energy visual waypoints.
- **Ember Energy**: Scorched oranges and molten vermilions illuminate key interactions, echoing flame-seared crusts and highway brake lights against asphalt.
- **Instrument-Grade Precision**: Crisp geometric dividers, structured data telemetry, and clear semantic wayfinding provide the cognitive clarity of high-end navigation systems.
- **Sensory Immersion**: Content layers evoke the tactile heat of premium smokehouses without resorting to literal skeuomorphism, relying instead on razor-sharp contrast, controlled luminescence, and restrained surface stepping.

## Colors

The color palette establishes an uncompromising dark-mode environment designed for high ambient-light legibility during night travel, paired with vivid culinary warmth.

### Primary & Interaction Accents
- **Primary Ember (`#F97316`)**: The primary brand driver, used for dominant CTAs, active route lines, active tabs, and critical focus states.
- **Ember Deep (`#EA580C`)**: Pressed and hover states for primary controls; border accents on active high-priority cards.

### Functional & Semantic Roles
- **Optimal Emerald (`#10B981`)**: Positive affirmations, open status indicators, prime meat cuts, optimal route efficiency, and top-tier rating badges.
- **Coral Hazard (`#EF4444`)**: Route detours, heavy traffic incidents, closed venues, critical warnings, and negative culinary attributes.
- **Amber Caution (`#F59E0B`)**: Slowdowns, moderate delays, closing-soon alerts, and medium rating brackets.

### Structural Neutrals & Surfaces
- **Obsidian Canvas (`#0F1115`)**: Base canvas for the entire viewport; minimizes eye fatigue and maximizes contrast.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#181B20`)**: Standard card bodies, navigation bars, bottom sheets, and secondary panels.
- **Surface Level 2 (`#22262E`)**: Elevated cards, inner modules, hovered rows, and modal dialogs.
- **Crisp Structural Border (`#2A2F3A`)**: Structural dividing lines, card outlines, and component frames.
- **Subtle Surface Overlay (`rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.08)`)**: Tonal tinting for interactive selection states.
- **Text High-Emphasis (`#F8FAFC`)**: Primary headlines, values, ratings, and essential metadata.
- **Text Medium-Emphasis (`#94A3B8`)**: Labels, secondary navigation items, and driving distance descriptions.
- **Text Low-Emphasis (`#64748B`)**: Inactive states, placeholder values, and timestamp dividers.

## Typography

The typography system relies on an architectural pairing of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for commanding, geometric headlines and **Inter** for neutral, hyper-legible body text and numerical telemetry.

### Tabular Numerical Data Rule
All review counts, travel times, mileage distances, speed indicators, and star ratings must enforce tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums;` or `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1;`). This maintains vertical alignment across rapidly updating travel statistics and comparison matrices.

### Scale Application
- **Headline XL / XL Mobile**: Reserved for destination arrival screens, city discovery titles, and hero map headers.
- **Headline LG & MD**: Restaurant names, waypoint destinations, and primary section breaks.
- **Telemetry LG & MD**: Applied specifically to trip duration badges (e.g., `18 min`, `4.2 mi`), BBQ smoking times, and pricing values.
- **Label SM**: Uppercase badges, category tickers, and status tags with wide tracking for legibility against dark slate fills.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a disciplined 4px base unit, scaling across a flexible grid optimized for simultaneous map browsing and telemetry listing.

### Grid Hierarchy
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column fluid layout with `margin: 1rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. Bottom sheets collapse down to reveal high-density interactive map canvases.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px)**: 8-column layout with `margin-md: 1.5rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. Employs a split-screen viewport: 40% persistent route overview, 60% scrollable restaurant and stop feed.
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12-column layout pinned to a maximum content width of 1440px with `margin-lg: 2.5rem` and `gutter-lg: 1.5rem`. Left navigation rails dock alongside deep-slate analytical telemetry cards.

### Spacing Rules
- Use `space-xs` and `space-sm` for pairing tightly bound metadata (e.g., star icons adjacent to rating scores, speed warnings).
- Use `space-md` for standard component padding within card containers and input groupings.
- Use `space-lg` and `space-xl` strictly for delineating separate route checkpoints, category shelves, and distinct search clusters.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on heavy drop shadows, which muddy dark canvas viewports. Depth is achieved via architectural surface layering, razor-sharp perimeter framing, and subtle localized ember luminosity.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Ground Canvas - `#0F1115`)**: Vector map rendering, backdrops, and root app canvas.
- **Tier 1 (Resting Substrates - `#181B20`)**: Standard persistent shelves, non-active stop items, side panels, and primary list items. Bordered by a 1px solid stroke of `#2A2F3A`.
- **Tier 2 (Floating & Active - `#22262E`)**: Active route cards, popovers, pinned search overlays, and dropdown menus. Encased in a 1px stroke of `#2A2F3A` with an ambient shadow of `0 12px 28px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.

### Ember Illumination & State Depth
Active focus and priority route segments emit an ambient thermal glow rather than standard drop shadows:
- **Ember Focus Glow**: `0 0 0 1px #F97316, 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.25)`.
- **Emerald Optimal Accent**: `0 0 0 1px #10B981, 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.2)`.
- All modals and critical alerts sit over an Obsidian Scrim (`rgba(15, 17, 21, 0.85)`) paired with a `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)` treatment to maintain route geometry underneath without distracting from prompt actions.

## Shapes

The interface balances automotive ergonomics with precision digital tooling. The system adheres to `roundedness: 2` (0.5rem base radius):

- **Base Components (0.5rem / 8px)**: Buttons, text inputs, telemetry pill containers, and review breakdown bars.
- **Large Components (1rem / 16px)**: Route summary cards, food stop previews, venue gallery modals, and floating panels.
- **Extra Large Overlays (1.5rem / 24px)**: Top/bottom sheets on mobile devices and primary bottom action sheets.
- **Full Radius (Pill / 9999px)**: Live indicators, active status tags (`OPEN NOW`, `OFF-ROUTE`), driving-time chips, and circular map pin icons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Background `#F97316`, label `#FFFFFF` in `label-lg`, border `none`. Hover state shifts to `#EA580C` with `0 4px 16px rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.3)`. Active state applies `scale(0.98)`.
- **Secondary / Surface**: Background `#181B20`, border `1px solid #2A2F3A`, label `#F8FAFC`. Hover state shifts background to `#22262E` with border `#F97316`.
- **Destructive / Reroute**: Background `transparent`, border `1px solid #EF4444`, label `#EF4444`. Hover state applies `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.1)` fill.

### Chips & Waypoint Badges
- **Status Chips**: Height of 28px, shape `pill`, padding `0 10px`. Background `#181B20` with a 1px border matching the semantic indicator.
- **Optimal Route Chip**: Background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`, text `#10B981`, border `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)`.
- **Delay / Traffic Chip**: Background `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.12)`, text `#EF4444`, border `1px solid rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.3)`.

### Cards (Culinary & Route Stops)
- Built on `#181B20` with a 1px `#2A2F3A` border and `rounded-lg` (16px) corners.
- Images feature a subtle linear vignette overlay (`linear-gradient(180deg, transparent 60%, #181B20 100%)`) allowing text to overlap safely.
- Internal cards feature split metadata columns with tabular numbers: drive distance (`Inter` tabular), smokehouse rating stars, and price bracket (`$$$`).

### Input Fields
- Height of 48px for touch target confidence during navigation. Background `#0F1115`, border `1px solid #2A2F3A`, typography `body-md`, placeholder text `#64748B`.
- Focused state transitions border to `#F97316` accompanied by an ember-toned box-shadow ring (`0 0 0 1px #F97316`).

### Checkboxes & Toggle Switches
- Checkboxes use `rounded: 4px` on `#181B20` with `1px solid #2A2F3A`. Selected state fills with `#F97316` featuring a stark white checkmark icon.
- Toggles utilize an obsidian track (`#0F1115`) with a 1px border. The active toggle knob glows `#F97316` and glides along an `#EA580C` track fill.

### Telemetry Displays & Route Progress
- **Distance & Time Pill**: High-contrast `#22262E` backdrop showcasing dynamic ETA using `telemetry-lg` alongside bold emerald or coral travel deltas.
- **Meat Cut & Smoke Type Gauges**: Micro-bar meters showing heat levels and wood smoke types using segmented track bars filled with `#F97316` against `#2A2F3A`.
